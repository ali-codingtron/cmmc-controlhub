Repair and professionally redesign ONLY the Control HUB Implementation Roadmap module.

I provided a video showing the current workflow.

The Implementation Roadmap contains functional, visual, progress-calculation, and organization-configuration issues.

Observed issues:

1. The visual design uses too many unrelated pastel colors across phase cards, banners, statistics, tabs, badges, progress sections, and action cards.

2. An action can be manually set to Complete even though the page shows blocking conditions such as:
   - Overview not acknowledged
   - Required steps incomplete
   - Required evidence not uploaded or linked
   - Validation not completed

3. The “Where You Are” tracker may display Review & Complete as the current stage even when earlier stages are incomplete.

4. Roadmap Progress can show 100% based primarily on manually selected action statuses even when the underlying implementation requirements are incomplete.

5. Selecting Upload New from Evidence to Collect leaves the roadmap and opens a generic `/evidence/upload` page.

6. The generic upload page is not functional:
   - no usable file-selection workflow
   - roadmap action context is lost
   - evidence requirement context is lost
   - linked controls are not preselected
   - no return to the original action
   - the upload does not reliably satisfy the roadmap evidence requirement

7. Link Existing opens a narrow, low-contrast modal:
   - Link buttons are visually unclear
   - selection state is unclear
   - the modal is difficult to scan
   - evidence metadata is insufficient
   - there is no prominent final “Link Selected Evidence” action
   - the list is cramped and relies on a narrow internal scrollbar

8. The action-detail page has excessive blank space and an awkwardly detached assignment panel.

9. Empty phases can show confusing values such as `0/0 Complete`.

10. Some organizations may not need the Implementation Roadmap, but the module is always visible.

Goal:

Make the Implementation Roadmap:

- visually restrained and professional
- functionally connected to Evidence
- accurate in its progress calculations
- impossible to mark Complete accidentally
- easy to navigate
- optional per organization
- safe for existing production data

Status behavior:

Not Started:
No work has begun.

In Progress:
Overview acknowledged or at least one step started.

Evidence Needed:
Required configuration steps are complete, but evidence or documents are missing.

Ready for Review:

Complete:

Blocked:
A recorded issue prevents progress.

Not Applicable:
Requires justification and authorized approval.

4. Validate
5. Review & Complete

Stage 1 — Understand

Complete when:

Stage 2 — Perform Steps

- every required step is Complete or approved Not Applicable

Stage 3 — Upload Evidence

- every required evidence requirement has at least one qualifying linked Evidence record

- stages 1–4 are complete
- required documents are satisfied
- required checklist items are complete
- no unresolved blockers remain

Do not highlight Stage 5 merely because the manual action status currently says Complete.
