Create a package-driven CMMC Level 1 Annual Self-Assessment module using Control HUB’s 17-requirement CMMC Level 1 operating model and a complete FAR 52.204-21 crosswalk.

IMPORTANT DESIGN DECISION:

Control HUB will use CMMC Level 1 as the primary user-facing package.

The application will contain:

- 17 operational CMMC Level 1 assessment requirements
- 15 FAR 52.204-21 safeguarding requirements
- one authoritative implementation and evidence record for each of the 17 CMMC assessment requirements
- a roll-up mapping from the 17 CMMC assessment requirements to the 15 FAR safeguarding requirements

Do not create a second duplicate set of implementation narratives, evidence, statuses, tasks, or assessment findings for FAR 52.204-21.

CMMC Level 1 is the primary Control HUB assessment model.

FAR 52.204-21 is the authoritative source and reporting crosswalk.

7. FAR ROLL-UP LOGIC
====================================================================

The 17 CMMC requirement findings are the primary assessment results.

Calculate the 15 FAR clause results from those findings.

For FAR clauses with one mapped CMMC requirement:

FAR clause result equals the mapped CMMC requirement result.

For FAR clause 52.204-21(b)(1)(ix):

The FAR clause is satisfied only when all three mapped CMMC requirements are:

- MET
or
- valid NOT APPLICABLE

The three mapped requirements are:

- Visitor Escort and Monitoring
- Physical Access Logs
- Physical Access Devices

If any of the three is NOT MET:

FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(ix) = NOT MET

If any of the three is Not Yet Assessed:

FAR 52.204-21(b)(1)(ix) = Not Yet Assessed

Do not average the three findings.

Do not calculate a numeric percentage for the FAR clause.

30. MOST IMPORTANT RULES
====================================================================

1. Control HUB uses 17 CMMC Level 1 requirements as its primary operational assessment model.

2. FAR 52.204-21 remains the authoritative source and 15-clause reporting crosswalk.

3. Do not create duplicate FAR implementation records.

4. All 17 CMMC findings must be completed before finalization.

5. The 15 FAR results are calculated from the mapped 17 CMMC findings.

6. FAR clause 52.204-21(b)(1)(ix) rolls up from three separate CMMC operational requirements.

7. Level 1 is assessed annually.

8. Do not create a CMMC assessment POA&M or Conditional Level 1 status.

9. Prior annual assessments remain immutable historical snapshots.
