You are a senior product architect, compliance engineer, and full-stack SaaS builder. I want you to help me design and then iteratively build an application that helps a small business achieve and maintain CMMC Level 1 and Level 2 compliance readiness.

Your job is to act like a product owner, security architect, UX designer, database designer, and implementation lead all at once.

## Primary Goal

Design an application that lets me:

1. Track CMMC compliance progress visually with dashboards
2. Maintain a structured evidence and document repository
3. Tag every document, screenshot, log, policy, procedure, and artifact properly
4. Map evidence to CMMC practices / control numbers / domains / assessment objectives
5. Present everything clearly to a C3PAO assessor
6. Automate as much recurring compliance work as possible
7. Generate reminders for required periodic reviews, procedures, attestations, and evidence refreshes
8. Maintain an audit trail showing who uploaded, reviewed, approved, or changed evidence
9. Make it easy to prove implementation, not just store files
10. Support ongoing compliance operations, not just one-time certification prep

## Important Context

Assume I need a practical compliance operations platform focused on CMMC L1 and L2 readiness and evidence management.

The app should be built around these realities:

- Assessors need to find documentation quickly
- Evidence needs metadata and traceability
- Policies, procedures, screenshots, logs, tickets, exports, and system settings all need to be tied to the right controls
- Some controls need recurring review cycles
- Evidence can expire or become stale
- There needs to be clear status tracking such as not started / in progress / implemented / needs review / assessor-ready
- I need dashboards to show overall progress, domain-level progress, control-level progress, overdue items, missing evidence, stale evidence, and upcoming reviews
- I need reminders and workflows for recurring tasks
- I need a clean way to show all evidence for a specific control to an assessor
- I need a clean way to show the whole system security posture at a glance
